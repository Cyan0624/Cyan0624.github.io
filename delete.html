How to delete your account and data (English)

Quoth is operated by Jo Yongwoo.

  • In the app: open Quoth, go to Settings, then Account, then "계정 삭제" (Delete account), and confirm with your password. Your account and server backup are deleted.
  • By email: if you cannot use the app, email 00ffff4293@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Quoth account deletion request". We will verify the request and delete your account and backup without undue delay.
  • What is deleted: your account (email address, password) and your server backup (viewing history, one-line notes, saved questions, settings).
  • What stays: records stored only on your device are removed when you uninstall the app. Backups and logs held by Google may remain for a limited period under Google's policies.
  • If you never signed in, no data is stored on our servers.
